Summary

I am a Graph ML and computational biology researcher with experience building heterogeneous knowledge graphs, graph neural network models, and reproducible machine learning pipelines for therapeutic target discovery, drug repurposing, and translational biomarker research.

I built multi-omics graph workflows spanning 15+ biomedical databases, applied PyTorch-based graph learning methods to prioritize compounds and genes across complex diseases, and developed real-world data pipelines over electronic health records and genotype arrays for 400,000+ participants in the All of Us Research Program.

Career Highlights

  • Built a heterogeneous multi-omics knowledge graph integrating gene-disease, compound-gene, compound-disease, and protein-protein interaction evidence from 15+ biomedical databases.

  • Developed a signed GNN model tracing cellular signatures of 3,000+ complex traits by linking PerturbSeq, UK Biobank PheWAS, and WES LoF/GoF summaries.

  • Built reproducible Python, Bash, Docker, and HPC/cloud workflows across transcriptomics, multimodal neuroimaging, and consortium-scale biomedical data.
