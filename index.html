Summary

I am a neuroimaging and computational psychiatry researcher building reproducible, ML-enabled pipelines across multimodal brain data, including resting-state fMRI, EEG, DTI, and PET.

My work focuses on biomarker discovery, cross-site model generalizability, and provenance-aware scientific automation. I develop end-to-end workflows that connect neuroimaging analysis with transcriptomic, genomic, and real-world clinical data to produce actionable insights for translational neuroscience.

Research Vision

Integrating multiple omics data with neuroimaging to better understand the brain in health and disease.
